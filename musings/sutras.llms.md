# Selected scriptures and treatises

There are thousands of Buddhist texts, spanning many vehicles, disciplines and traditions. Translating these texts in an ongoing endeavour. Below is a selection of websites that I have come across over the years and which I think are worth highlighting.

> **NOTE:**
>
> [SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net) contains early Buddhist texts, known as the Tipiṭaka or “Three Baskets”. This is a large collection of teachings attributed to the Buddha or his earliest disciples, who were teaching in India around 2500 years ago. They are regarded as sacred canon in all schools of Buddhism.
>
> - [The Characteristic of Nonself. *Anattalakkhaṇasutta.*](https://suttacentral.net/sn22.59/en/sujato?lang=en&layout=plain&notes=asterisk&highlight=false&script=latin)
> - [The Shorter Discourse on Emptiness. *Cūḷasuññatasutta.*](https://suttacentral.net/mn121/en/sujato?lang=en&layout=plain&notes=asterisk&highlight=false&script=latin)

> **NOTE:**
>
> [84000](https://84000.co) is dedicated to bringing the entire Tibetan Buddhist canon to the world—translated, preserved, and freely available to anyone. The [reading room](https://84000.co/reading-room) contains the Kangyur (the teachings of the Buddha) and the Tengyur (commentaries, philosophical works, and scholarly texts by Indian and Tibetan masters that illuminate the Buddhist teachings). Note that many texts are still in the process of being translated.
>
> - [The Stem Array. *Gaṇḍa­vyūha.*](https://84000.co/translation/toh44-45)
> - [Unraveling the Intent. *Saṃdhi­nirmocana.*](https://84000.co/translation/toh106)
> - [Lotus Sutra. *Saddharma­puṇḍarīka.*](https://84000.co/translation/toh113)
> - [The Perfection of Wisdom in Seven Hundred Lines. *Saptaśatikānāma­prajñāpāramitā.*](https://84000.co/translation/toh24)

> **NOTE:**
>
> [Studybuddhism.com](https://studybuddhism.com/) is an extensive source of authentic Buddhist teachings, presented in a down-to-earth and practical way. This website is the next generation of The Berzin Archives, founded in 2001 by Dr. Alexander Berzin, a Buddhist teacher, translator and practitioner with more than 50 years of experience.
>

> - [Nagarjuna: Letter to a Friend.](https://studybuddhism.com/en/tibetan-buddhism/original-texts/sutra-texts/letter-to-a-friend)
